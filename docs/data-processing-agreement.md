# Data Processing Agreement

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** [Date]

---

## Parties

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between:

**Controller:** The entity identified in the Genesis subscription agreement ("Client" or "Controller")

**Processor:** Genesis ("Genesis" or "Processor")

This DPA forms part of and is subject to the terms of the Genesis subscription agreement between the parties (the "Principal Agreement").

---

## 1. Definitions

**"Data Protection Laws"** means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and any other applicable data protection legislation in the United Kingdom.

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined in Data Protection Laws.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, analysis, use, disclosure, and deletion.

**"Data Subject"** means an identified or identifiable natural person whose Personal Data is processed.

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

**"Security Incident"** means any accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.

**"Standard Contractual Clauses"** or **"SCCs"** means the standard contractual clauses approved by the UK Information Commissioner for international data transfers.

## 2. Scope and Purpose of Processing

### 2.1 Subject Matter

The Processor shall process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller for the purpose of providing the Genesis service as described in the Principal Agreement.

### 2.2 Nature of Processing

The Processor will:

- Receive LinkedIn content export data uploaded by the Controller
- Analyse content performance against Controller's declared intent
- Generate Genesis Profiles summarising findings
- Store and transmit data as necessary to provide the service

### 2.3 Purpose

Processing is performed solely for:

- Generating Genesis Profiles for the Controller
- Identifying patterns in content performance
- Providing insights related to the Controller's declared goals

### 2.4 Duration

Processing shall continue for the duration of the Principal Agreement and for such additional period as necessary to fulfil the Processor's obligations under this DPA.

### 2.5 Data Subjects

Data Subjects may include:

- Controller's employees who create LinkedIn content
- Controller's LinkedIn audience (aggregated engagement metrics only—not individual profiles)

### 2.6 Categories of Personal Data

- LinkedIn post content and metadata
- Post performance metrics (impressions, engagement, clicks)
- Timing and format information
- Controller's declared intent and goal statements

The full description of processing is set out in **Annex A**.

## 3. Obligations of the Processor

### 3.1 Processing Instructions

The Processor shall:

(a) Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required to do so by applicable law

(b) Immediately inform the Controller if, in the Processor's opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws

(c) Not process Personal Data for any purpose other than providing the Genesis service

### 3.2 Confidentiality

The Processor shall:

(a) Ensure that all personnel authorised to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations

(b) Limit access to Personal Data to personnel who require access to perform the service

### 3.3 Security

The Processor shall:

(a) Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as detailed in **Annex B**

(b) Regularly test, assess, and evaluate the effectiveness of security measures

(c) Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with security obligations under Data Protection Laws

### 3.4 Sub-processing

The Processor shall:

(a) Not engage a Sub-processor without prior written authorisation from the Controller

(b) The Controller provides general authorisation for the Sub-processors listed in **Annex C**

(c) Notify the Controller of any intended changes to Sub-processors, giving the Controller 30 days to object

(d) Ensure Sub-processors are bound by data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA

(e) Remain fully liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors

### 3.5 Data Subject Rights

The Processor shall:

(a) Promptly notify the Controller upon receiving a request from a Data Subject regarding their rights

(b) Assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests, taking into account the nature of processing

(c) Not respond directly to Data Subject requests unless authorised by the Controller or required by law

### 3.6 Security Incidents

The Processor shall:

(a) Notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, upon becoming aware of a Security Incident

(b) Provide the Controller with sufficient information to enable the Controller to comply with its notification obligations

(c) Cooperate with the Controller in investigating and mitigating the Security Incident

(d) Not inform any third party of a Security Incident without the Controller's prior consent, unless required by law

### 3.7 Data Protection Impact Assessments

The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Controller with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities, where required by Data Protection Laws.

### 3.8 Deletion and Return

Upon termination of the Principal Agreement or upon request:

(a) The Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all Personal Data

(b) Deletion shall be completed within 30 days of request

(c) The Processor shall certify deletion in writing upon request

(d) The Processor may retain Personal Data where required by applicable law, notifying the Controller of such requirement

## 4. Obligations of the Controller

### 4.1 Lawful Basis

The Controller warrants that:

(a) It has a lawful basis for providing Personal Data to the Processor

(b) It has provided any necessary notices to Data Subjects

(c) It has obtained any necessary consents for the processing described in this DPA

The Controller shall ensure that Personal Data provided to the Processor is accurate and up to date.

### 4.3 Instructions

The Controller shall:

(a) Provide documented instructions for processing Personal Data

(b) Ensure instructions comply with Data Protection Laws

(c) Be responsible for the lawfulness of its instructions

### 4.4 Security

The Controller shall implement appropriate security measures for any Personal Data in its possession or control before transfer to the Processor.

## 5. Sub-processors

### 5.1 Authorised Sub-processors

The Controller authorises the use of Sub-processors listed in **Annex C** as of the effective date of this DPA.

### 5.2 Changes to Sub-processors

(a) The Processor shall notify the Controller via email at least 30 days before engaging a new Sub-processor or replacing an existing Sub-processor

(b) The Controller may object to such changes by notifying the Processor in writing within 14 days of receipt of notice

(c) If the Controller objects, the parties shall discuss the objection in good faith. If agreement cannot be reached, the Controller may terminate the affected services without penalty

### 5.3 Sub-processor Agreements

The Processor shall enter into written agreements with each Sub-processor containing data protection obligations substantially similar to those in this DPA.

## 6. Data Subject Rights

### 6.1 Cooperation

The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Data Protection Laws, including:

(a) Access to Personal Data
(b) Rectification of inaccurate data
(c) Erasure of Personal Data
(d) Restriction of processing
(e) Data portability
(f) Objection to processing

### 6.2 Response Time

The Processor shall respond to Controller requests for assistance within 5 business days.

### 6.3 Costs

Where assistance requires significant resources, the Processor may charge reasonable costs at rates agreed in advance with the Controller.

## 7. Security Measures

### 7.1 Technical and Organisational Measures

The Processor shall implement and maintain the technical and organisational security measures described in **Annex B**.

### 7.2 Updates

The Processor may update security measures from time to time, provided that such updates do not materially reduce the overall level of protection.

---

## 8. Data Breach Notification

### 8.1 Notification Timing

Upon becoming aware of a Security Incident, the Processor shall notify the Controller:

(a) Without undue delay
(b) In any event within 48 hours

### 8.2 Notification Content

Notification shall include, to the extent known:

(a) A description of the nature of the Security Incident, including categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected

(b) The name and contact details of the Processor's point of contact

(c) The likely consequences of the Security Incident

(d) Measures taken or proposed to address the Security Incident and mitigate its effects

### 8.3 Ongoing Communication

The Processor shall provide further information as it becomes available and cooperate with the Controller's investigation.

### 8.4 Documentation

The Processor shall document all Security Incidents, including facts, effects, and remedial actions taken.

---

## 9. Data Transfers

### 9.1 Transfers Within the UK and EU

The Processor may process Personal Data within the United Kingdom and European Economic Area without additional safeguards.

### 9.2 Transfers Outside the UK and EU

For transfers to countries not subject to an adequacy decision:

(a) The Processor shall ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses

(b) The Processor shall provide the Controller with copies of relevant transfer mechanisms upon request

(c) Current international transfers and their safeguards are detailed in **Annex C**

### 9.3 Changes to Transfer Mechanisms

If a transfer mechanism is invalidated, the Processor shall promptly implement alternative safeguards or cease the affected transfer.

---

## 10. Audit Rights

### 10.1 Documentation

The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

### 10.2 Audit Reports

Upon request (no more than once per year), the Processor shall provide:

(a) Copies of relevant security certifications
(b) Summaries of recent penetration test results
(c) Evidence of compliance with security measures in Annex B

### 10.3 On-site Audits

(a) The Controller may conduct on-site audits upon 30 days written notice

(b) Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours with minimal disruption

(c) The Controller shall bear the costs of on-site audits

(d) Auditors shall be bound by confidentiality obligations

### 10.4 Scope

Audits shall be limited to processing activities relating to the Controller's Personal Data.

---

## 11. Duration and Termination

### 11.1 Duration

This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the Principal Agreement.

### 11.2 Survival

Obligations relating to confidentiality, data deletion, and audit rights shall survive termination.

### 11.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:

(a) The Processor shall cease processing Personal Data except as necessary to return or delete it

(b) The Processor shall comply with deletion or return obligations within 30 days

---

## 12. Liability

### 12.1 Liability Cap

The total liability of either party under this DPA shall be subject to the limitations of liability set out in the Principal Agreement.

### 12.2 Exclusions

Nothing in this DPA shall limit liability for:

(a) Fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation
(b) Death or personal injury caused by negligence
(c) Any other liability that cannot be limited by law

### 12.3 Indemnification

Each party shall indemnify the other for losses arising from the indemnifying party's breach of this DPA or Data Protection Laws.

---

## 13. General Provisions

### 13.1 Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.

### 13.2 Jurisdiction

The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising from this DPA.

### 13.3 Amendments

This DPA may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

### 13.4 Entire Agreement

This DPA, together with the Principal Agreement and its Annexes, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding data processing.

### 13.5 Conflict

In the event of conflict between this DPA and the Principal Agreement, this DPA shall prevail with respect to data protection matters.

# Annex B: Technical and Organisational Measures

## B.1 Encryption

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Encryption at rest | AES-256 encryption for all stored data |
| Encryption in transit | TLS 1.3 for all data transmission |
| Key management | Keys stored separately from encrypted data, rotated annually |

## B.2 Access Controls

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Authentication | Multi-factor authentication required for all access |
| Authorisation | Role-based access control (RBAC) |
| Principle of least privilege | Access limited to minimum necessary |
| Access reviews | Quarterly review of access permissions |
| Offboarding | Immediate access revocation upon role change or termination |

## B.3 Network Security

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Firewalls | Web application firewall (WAF) protecting all endpoints |
| Network segmentation | Production, staging, and development environments isolated |
| DDoS protection | Cloud-based DDoS mitigation |
| Intrusion detection | Automated monitoring for suspicious activity |

## B.4 Application Security

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Secure development | Security requirements in development lifecycle |
| Code review | Mandatory peer review for all code changes |
| Dependency scanning | Automated scanning for vulnerable dependencies |
| Input validation | Validation of all user inputs |
| API security | API authentication, rate limiting, input validation |

## B.5 Data Security

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Data classification | Four-tier classification (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) |
| Data minimisation | Collection limited to data necessary for service |
| Pseudonymisation | Where possible, identifying information is pseudonymised |
| Secure deletion | Cryptographic erasure for deleted data |

## B.6 Physical Security

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Data centre security | SOC 2 Type II certified data centres |
| Physical access | Multi-layer access controls at hosting facilities |
| Environmental controls | Fire suppression, climate control, redundant power |

## B.7 Operational Security

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Logging | Comprehensive audit logging of all access and changes |
| Monitoring | 24/7 monitoring for security events |
| Patch management | Critical patches applied within 7 days |
| Backup | Daily encrypted backups with 90-day retention |
| Disaster recovery | Recovery point objective (RPO): 24 hours; Recovery time objective (RTO): 4 hours |

## B.8 Personnel Security

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Background checks | Pre-employment verification for all staff |
| Training | Annual security awareness training |
| Confidentiality | Binding confidentiality agreements |
| Acceptable use | Documented acceptable use policy |

## B.9 Incident Response

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Incident response plan | Documented procedures for detection, containment, eradication, recovery |
| Response team | Designated incident response team with defined roles |
| Communication | Pre-defined communication templates and escalation paths |
| Post-incident review | Mandatory review within 7 days of incident resolution |

## B.10 Vendor Management

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Due diligence | Security assessment before engaging vendors |
| Contractual requirements | Data protection terms in all vendor contracts |
| Ongoing monitoring | Annual review of vendor security posture |
