Before the legal detail, here's what matters:

## 2. What Data We Collect

### Account Data

When you create an account, we collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your company name (if provided)
- Your password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text)

### LinkedIn Export Data

When you use Genesis, you upload LinkedIn data exports containing:

- Your LinkedIn posts and their content
- Post performance metrics (impressions, engagement, clicks)
- Posting dates and times
- Post format information

This data comes from LinkedIn's export feature—we don't access your LinkedIn account directly.

### Intent Declaration Data

We collect information about your stated goals:

- Your content objectives
- Your target audience descriptions
- Success criteria you define

### Usage Data

We automatically collect:

- How you interact with Genesis (pages visited, features used)
- Your device type and browser
- Your IP address (used for security and approximate location)
- Timestamps of your activity

### Payment Data

If you subscribe to a paid plan:

- Payment is processed by Stripe
- We receive confirmation of payment status
- We do not store your card number, CVV, or full card details

## 3. Why We Collect It (Legal Bases)

Under UK GDPR, we need a legal basis to process your personal data. Here are the bases we rely on:

### Contract Performance

We process your account data and LinkedIn export data to provide the Genesis service. This is necessary to fulfil our contract with you.

### Legitimate Interests

We process usage data to:

- Improve Genesis and fix problems
- Protect against fraud and abuse
- Understand how people use our service

We've assessed that these interests don't override your rights.

### Consent

We only send marketing communications if you've opted in. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking unsubscribe or contacting us.

## 4. How We Use Your Data

### To Generate Your Genesis Profile

We analyse your LinkedIn content and performance data against your declared intent to produce your Genesis Profile. This is the core service.

### To Identify Patterns

We look for patterns in your content performance to show you what approaches consistently work (or don't) for the right reasons.

### To Improve Our Service

We use aggregated, anonymised data to improve our analysis methods. Individual user data is not used for this purpose without anonymisation.

### To Communicate With You

We send:

- Your Genesis Profile (the service you signed up for)
- Account notifications (password resets, plan changes)
- Service updates (new features, maintenance)
- Marketing communications (only if you've opted in)

### To Comply With Law

We may process your data to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests from authorities, or protect our legal rights.

## 5. Who We Share Data With

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. We share it only with:

### Cloud Infrastructure (Supabase)

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the EU (Frankfurt region). Supabase provides database and authentication services.

### Analysis Processing (Anthropic)

Your LinkedIn content is processed using Anthropic's analysis services to generate your Genesis Profile. Important: Your data is not used to train Anthropic's models. This is governed by our data processing agreement with Anthropic.

### Payment Processing (Stripe)

Stripe handles payment processing. They receive payment information directly—we don't pass your card details through our systems.

### Email Delivery (SendGrid)

SendGrid delivers emails including your Genesis Profile and account notifications.

### Legal Requirements

We may share data with law enforcement or regulators if required by law or to protect against harm.

## 6. How Long We Keep Your Data

### Active Accounts

While your account is active, we retain your data to provide the service.

### Cancelled Accounts

After you cancel:

- Your data is retained for 30 days to allow for reactivation or data export
- After 30 days, your data is deleted unless you request it sooner

### Automatic Purge

Accounts with no activity for 24 months are flagged for deletion. We'll notify you before deleting inactive accounts.

### Backups

Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days after deletion from production systems for disaster recovery purposes.

### Legal Holds

If we're required to retain data for legal reasons, we'll keep it as long as necessary.

## 7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

### Access Your Data

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. We'll provide it within 30 days.

### Correct Your Data

If your data is inaccurate or incomplete, ask us to correct it.

### Delete Your Data

Request deletion of your personal data. We'll delete it unless we have a legal obligation to keep it.

### Export Your Data

Receive your data in a portable format (CSV/JSON) to transfer to another service.

### Object to Processing

Object to processing based on legitimate interests. We'll stop unless we have compelling grounds to continue.

### Restrict Processing

Ask us to restrict processing while we address a complaint or verify the accuracy of your data.

### Withdraw Consent

If we process data based on consent (like marketing emails), withdraw that consent at any time.

### Complain to a Regulator

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

**Website:** ico.org.uk
**Phone:** 0303 123 1113

### How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at privacy@genesis.so with your request. We'll respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing requests.
