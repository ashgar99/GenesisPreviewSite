The Problem

Teams have data. Teams have AI.
Teams still can't judge outcomes.

Most "wins" are coincidences that get promoted to strategy. Most "failures" contain learning that gets ignored.

Vanity metrics everywhere

Dashboards show what happened. They don't tell you if it happened for the right reasons.

Reactive decision-making

Teams double down on what "worked" without asking whether it was skill or luck.

No shared judgement standard

Everyone has opinions about what's working. No one has a framework to verify.

Dual Intelligence

Two models. One truth.

Genesis uses two distinct models that work together—one for memory, one for judgement.

RIM

Retrospective Intelligence Model

"What patterns exist in what we've done, and which are trustworthy?"

  • Aggregates historical actions and outcomes
  • Identifies repeatable patterns vs one-off successes
  • Flags false positives (noise mistaken as signal)
  • Establishes baselines for future judgement
IEM

Intent-Execution Model

"Did this action behave the way it was designed to behave?"

  • Tests executions against stated intent
  • Distinguishes designed success from coincidence
  • Produces pass / fail / uncertain verdicts
  • Generates reasoning traces for every decision
Intent Within Execution

Know why things work,
not just that they do

Genesis tests whether your actions achieved their intended purpose—not just whether they got engagement.

  • Declare intent upfront

    Before you execute, define what success actually looks like.

  • Get honest verdicts

    Genesis tells you when success was accidental—even if the numbers look good.

  • Learn what actually works

    Only validated learnings inform strategy. Noise is blocked.

The Four Verdicts

Every action gets a judgement

Clear classifications. No ambiguity. Each verdict tells you exactly what to do next.

Pass

Success by design

The action achieved its intended purpose. The outcome matched the expected markers.

✓ Informs RIM
Fail

Clean miss

Good execution, wrong outcome. The action didn't achieve what it was designed to do.

✓ Informs RIM (negative signal)
Coincidence

Success by accident

The numbers look good, but for the wrong reasons. This shouldn't inform your strategy.

✗ Blocked until recalibration
Uncertain

Insufficient signal

Not enough data to make a judgement. Genesis won't guess—it waits for clarity.

✗ Blocked until resolved
