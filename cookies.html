Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

The Short Version

  • We use minimal, essential cookies only
  • No tracking cookies or third-party advertising
  • No cookie consent banner needed because we only use strictly necessary cookies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, making subsequent visits easier and the site more useful.

How We Use Cookies

Genesis takes a minimal approach to cookies. We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to function. We don't use cookies for advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to work and cannot be switched off.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
session_id Keeps you logged in during your visit Session
csrf_token Security: prevents cross-site request forgery Session
lang_preference Remembers your language selection 1 year

What We Don't Use

Unlike many websites, Genesis does not use:

  • Analytics cookies: We don't track your behaviour across pages
  • Advertising cookies: We don't show ads or sell data to advertisers
  • Social media cookies: We don't embed social widgets that track you
  • Third-party tracking: We don't allow other companies to place cookies on our site

Managing Cookies

Since we only use essential cookies, there's no cookie banner to manage preferences. However, you can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Note: Blocking essential cookies may prevent Genesis from working properly.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our approach to cookies, we'll update this policy and notify you. We're committed to the minimal cookie approach and don't anticipate adding tracking or advertising cookies.
